How the numbers are built

Beat/Meet/Lose compares our cheapest offer with the cheapest of Check24, Camperdays and CU Camper per route, pick-up month and duration; weights are expected bookings (50% bookings, 40% seasonality, 10% basket). Price moves use the same matched vehicle-trips on both days. Extreme vehicle-trips compare each trip's gap with the median gap of the same vehicle and duration.
